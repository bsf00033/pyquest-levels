📜 Quick Lesson: Numbers & Math

Numbers don’t need quotes. Python can add, subtract, multiply, and divide them with the symbols + - * /.

+add -subtract *multiply /divide (gives a decimal, like 5.0)
# Mix two ingredients
herbs = 3
water = 2
print(herbs + water)
→ 5
# Multiply for bigger batches
print(4 * 5)
→ 20

Parentheses ( ) go first — just like in school math. Without them, * and / happen before + and -.

# Parentheses first!
print((2 + 3) * 4)
→ 20 # Without ( ): 2 + 3 * 4 → 14
