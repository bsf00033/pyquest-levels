📜 Quick Lesson: Numbers & attack math

Attack damage is a number — no quotes. You can subtract to find remaining HP and multiply for a charged strike.

# Attack power is a number
attack = 20
print(attack)
→ 20
# Remaining HP = enemy_hp minus damage
enemy_hp = 60
damage = 20
print(enemy_hp - damage)
→ 40
# Charged strike multiplies
base = 15
charge = 2
print(base * charge)
→ 30
− subtract
* multiply
+ add
